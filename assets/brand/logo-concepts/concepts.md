# Aster Office logo concepts

The app icons pair a near-black background with one accent; the transparent marks and lockups use near-black alone.

## concept-a - Page compass

Idea: Four clipped-corner pages point outward around a square of negative space, making a precise four-point document star.

Accent: **#93A0EB**, periwinkle.

Weakness: The fourfold silhouette can read as a navigation symbol before it reads as office software.

## concept-b - Cut apex

Idea: A solid geometric A uses a flat apex, a triangular counter, and a broad crossbar to suggest a stable, efficient desktop tool.

Accent: **#D99B78**, copper.

Weakness: The conventional A silhouette is less distinctive than the other symbols and offers little connection to the star or flower meaning.

## concept-c - Document masonry

Idea: A portrait page, a four-cell spreadsheet grid, and a landscape slide fit together into one compact arrangement of the suite's core document types.

Accent: **#B7B17A**, muted ochre.

Weakness: At 16 px the document details simplify into a pattern of blocks, so the office-suite meaning becomes less explicit.

## concept-d - Radial aster

Idea: Eight identical elliptical petals, placed at computed 45-degree intervals around a circular centre, reduce the aster flower to a balanced geometric silhouette.

Accent: **#B39AD7**, lavender.

Weakness: The flower has little inherent connection to document editing and may feel softer than the product's technical positioning.
